## 🧬 Our Mission
We are dedicated to the **digital restoration** and **algorithmic generation** of Somali microtonal music. Our work bridges the gap between historical archival materials (1950s–1990s) and modern machine learning architectures. We aim to solve the "Western bias" in Music Information Retrieval (MIR) by creating systems that natively understand the **Somali Pentatonic Scale** and the microtonal nuances of the **Oud (Kaban)**.

## 🔬 Core Technical Pillars

### 1. Automated Archival Restoration
We utilize advanced **source separation** to isolate the Oud from historical recordings characterized by noise and overlapping vocals. Our pipeline stabilizes analogue signals to create an "unadulterated" melodic stream for research.

### 2. Microtonal Attention Transformers
Unlike standard generative models that "crush" Somali neutral notes, our **QaraamiGen** architecture implements a custom attention mechanism to handle the modal structures and rhythmic "swings" stereotypical of the genre.

### 3. Human-AI Co-Creation
Inspired by the work of **Prof. Anna Huang**, we are building "Jam Spaces" where practitioners can define improvisation rules—modal focus, tempo, and qualitative density—to ensure culturally synchronistic outputs.
